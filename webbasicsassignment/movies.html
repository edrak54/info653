I watch a lot of movies, and recently I've been particulary interested in old school noir films. One of my favorite icebreaker questions to ask people is what the movie they've seen the most times is. I think it's more interesting than asking a favorite movie and it usually ends up being a favorite movie from childhood. Mine would be Looney Tunes: Back in Action (2003) which I watched a lot when I was younger.
